# Task-2
Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview
This project was developed as part of a Data Analyst Internship Task on **Data Visualization and Storytelling** using Power BI. The dashboard analyzes the Superstore dataset to identify sales trends, profit performance, customer segments, and regional business insights through interactive visualizations.

Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- Superstore Dataset (CSV)
- GitHub

Dashboard Pages

Page 1: Executive Overview
- KPI Cards for Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Quantity
- Sales by Region Analysis
- Customer Segment Distribution
- Executive Summary highlighting key business insights

Page 2: Sales & Profit Analysis
- Profit by Category (Treemap)
- Sales vs Profit by Category (Scatter Plot)
- State-wise Profit Analysis
- Profit Performance Comparison across states

Page 3: Business Storytelling
- Discount vs Profit Analysis
- Business Story Summary
- Strategic Recommendations for improving profitability and growth

Key Insights
- The West region generates the highest sales revenue.
- Consumer customers contribute the largest share of sales.
- Technology is the top-performing category in terms of sales and profit.
- California and New York are among the most profitable states.
- Higher discounts can negatively impact overall profitability.
Recommendations
- Focus marketing efforts on Technology products.
- Expand business operations in high-profit regions.
- Optimize discount strategies to improve profit margins.
- Prioritize profitable product categories for future growth.

This dashboard demonstrates how Power BI can be used to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making through effective visualization and storytelling.
